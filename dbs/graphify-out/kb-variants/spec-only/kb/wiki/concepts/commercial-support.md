# Commercial Support

Commercial support refers to paid assistance offered by a project vendor or maintainer organization to help users adopt, operate, and extend the software.

In the case of [[summaries/0005_licensing_d3fe8b66d2]], the licensing document notes that **commercial support for Ibex is available from lowRISC**. This means that, beyond the open-source license itself, organizations can seek a formal support relationship when they need help with integration, troubleshooting, or project-specific guidance.

## What it typically includes

- Implementation and integration help
- Bug investigation and troubleshooting
- Guidance for customization or product use
- Escalation paths for production issues
- Potential training or consulting services

## Why it matters

Commercial support is useful when a team needs:

- Faster responses than community channels can provide
- Confidence using the software in a commercial or mission-critical setting
- Expert advice from the organization closest to the project
- A way to budget for long-term maintenance and adoption

## In the source document

The licensing summary makes two related points:

1. Ibex is open source under the Apache 2.0 license, so it can be used freely.
2. Even with that permissive license, **commercial support is available from lowRISC**.

This combination is common in open-source ecosystems: the software remains openly licensed while professional support is offered separately.
